# Kit content strategy

Decided October 28, 2026. The full outline lives in Drive: "BBQ Hustle – Kit Outline & File Index".

## Structure
- **Build the Business** is one standalone package, written once and industry-neutral (tested). It is complete. It holds 12 modules, each followed by its own worksheet, plus two Google Sheets calculators shared as make-a-copy links.
- Each kit topic is its own separate package: BBQ Hustle (still being written), Farmstand Hustle and SideGig Hustle (not yet written).
- Every kit purchase delivers two packages: Build the Business and that kit's topic package. A buyer of a second kit gets only the new topic package.
- Build the Business is maintained in one place. A fix or update is made once and applies to every kit.

## Delivery
- The download page lists the files for the kit that was bought: 1. Build the Business, 2. the kit's topic package.
- Files are hosted in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket and served through personal expiring links. R2 is not switched on yet. Switching it on is on hold until the BBQ content is nearly finished.
- Suggested bucket layout: `shared/build-the-business.pdf`, plus one folder per kit (`bbq/`, `farmstand/`, `sidegig/`).
- If buyers prefer a single file, a combined PDF per kit can be generated later from the same sources.
- The confirmation email goes out through Resend from thebbqhustle.com. Each buyer is also added to HubSpot (free plan, customer list only) tagged by kit.

## Open ideas (not decided)
- A lower price on a second kit's topic package for existing Kit owners.

## Included with every kit purchase
- A personalized 30-day social media calendar, delivered within 24 to 48 hours.
- Access to the private Facebook group (not yet created).
